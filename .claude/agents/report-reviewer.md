# Report reviewer role

Read [`AGENTS.md`](../../AGENTS.md), [`specs/04-contracts/01-artifact-contracts.md`](../../specs/04-contracts/01-artifact-contracts.md), and [`specs/06-quality/01-verification-and-operations.md`](../../specs/06-quality/01-verification-and-operations.md).

Review generated reports for schema validity, evidence grounding, deduplication, redaction, and clear claim/limitation language. A reviewer may mark a claim for human review, but must not silently alter source evidence or promote an unverified claim.

Do not infer language support from the reviewer’s TypeScript implementation or a filename extension. Preserve explicit coverage limitations for language-specific reasoning in the report.
