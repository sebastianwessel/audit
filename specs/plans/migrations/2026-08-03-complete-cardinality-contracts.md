# Complete cardinality contracts

## Contract

Product repository tools, audit lineage, and evaluator artifacts must not reject or truncate complete declared collections because of a fixed product cardinality. This applies to include/exclude glob sets, lineage entries, per-trial plan/finding/review-required identities, readiness distributions/criteria/limitations, comparison incompatibilities, comparison stage rows, and evaluator-only acquisition metadata lanes, language sets, candidate registries, and advisory range collections.

Source-path validation is single-owned by the shared traversal-safe relative-path contract. It must never impose a product path-length ceiling or let a duplicate platform/model schema diverge from the jailed filesystem contract.

Source-free operational report projections follow the same rule: sort complete hotspot rows for usability, but do not retain a fixed-size prefix.

Optional artifact reads are single-owned by the output jail. Only `ENOENT` means an artifact is absent; malformed, unsafe, inaccessible, or otherwise unreadable artifacts fail closed. Resume separates reusable execution artifacts from every compatible observed model stage so unfinished prior work remains in the shared cost ledger without being reused as a terminal result.

Per-item validation, closed objects/enums, path-jail checks, exact finite enum projections, and operational timeouts remain in force. A product boundary may fail an invalid item or record incomplete coverage, but it may not use a fixed collection total as a shortcut for omission.

## Verification

- Unit tests parse collections above each retired ceiling and retain every item, including local acquisition metadata.
- Filesystem integration accepts more than 32 requested include globs without omitting an eligible file.
- Schema snapshots regenerate deterministically and the complete offline check stays green.
