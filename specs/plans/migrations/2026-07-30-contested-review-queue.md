# Contested review queue

The comparable five-repeat contradiction-barrier measurement reduced false positives from 10 to 5 but regressed true positives from 14 to 11. A candidate-blind posture is independent from the later candidate-aware review but is not a semantic oracle. Discarding a source-backed hypothesis on that disagreement creates false negatives; silently admitting it creates false-positive risk.

The terminal decision is therefore tri-state at the report boundary: verified findings are the only items eligible for priority-based CI gating; a source-backed, independently disputed hypothesis is persisted in the separate `reviewRequired` queue with `needs-review` and `insufficient-evidence`; rejected/incomplete hypotheses are not promoted. The queue preserves the redacted source-backed candidate and the reason for human adjudication, but never asserts a confirmed vulnerability, creates a chain, or affects the CI severity gate. It reuses the existing finding status and verification schemas rather than introducing a parallel type hierarchy.

This is generic disagreement preservation. It adds no language/parser/source/benchmark/answer-key logic, alternate model route, runtime attack behavior, or result-specific threshold. Vector checkpoints persist the queue alongside verified proposals and reports render a dedicated human-review section.

The compatible five-repeat `contested-review-queue-reviewed-20260730` measurement completed 30/30 trials using the same prompt protocol and route as `plan-obligation-reconciliation-reviewed-20260730`. Confirmed findings changed from 14 true positives, 10 false positives, and 6 false negatives to 13, 2, and 7 respectively; the release gate still failed. The review queue matched one expected vulnerable item, contained two non-vulnerable items, and left nineteen vulnerable expectations unmatched. It is therefore an observability and human-workflow improvement, not evidence of detection reliability or queue effectiveness. Do not tune prompts, source rules, language paths, thresholds, or answer-key behaviour against this seed. The next effectiveness work is corpus acquisition, independent adjudication, and a private holdout.
